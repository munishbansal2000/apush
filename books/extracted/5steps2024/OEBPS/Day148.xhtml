Day 148

In the 1998 film Pleasantville, the 1950s is presented as a time of repression, especially for African Americans and women. That women were all forced to be housewives is a widespread view of the era. Yet, by 1957 more women were working than during World War II. By 1960, 40 percent of women held jobs. Why does the less-complicated view of women’s roles in the 1950s persist? Why is this important?
