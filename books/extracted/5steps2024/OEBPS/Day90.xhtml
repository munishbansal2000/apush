Day 90

In 1873, Mark Twain and Charles Dudley Warner published a novel entitled The Gilded Age. The novel was a satire of contemporary America, condemning what the authors saw as a postwar riot of greed and public corruption. The title came from Shakespeare: “To gild refined gold, to paint the lily … is wasteful and ridiculous excess.” Twain and Warner achieved something unusual for novelists; they gave a name to a period of history. Why is the period of the late nineteenth century in the United States called the “Gilded Age”?
