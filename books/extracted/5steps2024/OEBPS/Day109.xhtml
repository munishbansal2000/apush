The American Battle Monuments Commission was established by Congress in 1923 to address an unprecedented issue for Americans—how to memorialize war dead who were buried overseas. The Commission created a system of cemeteries and war memorials notable for their beauty and artistic distinction. Explore the pages describing some of the World War I cemeteries and war memorials. What would have been some of the challenges facing those who were charged with creating these cemeteries and memorials?
