Day 28

Go to the website for Colonial Williamsburg: https://www.colonialwilliamsburg.com/.

Click on “Learn,” and then go to the “History” page. Look at the various pages there, paying particular attention to the portrayal of African Americans. Colonial Williamsburg attempts to portray colonial slavery accurately. Is this a good idea? Are there some aspects of the past that should not be re-created at popular historical and tourist sites?
