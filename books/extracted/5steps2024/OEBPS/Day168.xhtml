Day 168

“We gather tonight at a dramatic and deeply promising time in our history and the history of man on Earth. For in the past 12 months, the world has known changes of almost biblical proportions. … But the biggest thing that has happened in the world in my life, in our lives, is this: By the grace of God, America won the cold war.”

—President George H. W. Bush, “State of the Union Address,” January 28, 1992

President Bush in the passage above expressed the euphoria at the end of the cold war. Americans began to expect a “peace dividend” in the form of decreased defense spending. The scholar Francis Fukuyama published a widely discussed book entitled The End of History and the Last Man (1992) in which he argued that with the demise of Communism, humanity had reached the end of its ideological evolution, and the world would gradually adopt Western liberal democracy. Was Fukuyama’s prediction of an end of ideological struggle borne out by events?
