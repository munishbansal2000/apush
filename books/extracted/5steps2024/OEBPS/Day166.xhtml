Go to the website for the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art:

http://lucasmuseum.org/museum.

The Lucas Museum of Narrative Art features “visual art that tells a story.” It was endowed by George Lucas, the creator of Star Wars. Click on “The Collection,” and then “View the Art of Cinema.” Browse through the pages in this section, focusing on Star Wars and other movies of the 1970s and 1980s. Why were the films of directors like George Lucas and Steven Spielberg important to the culture of the late 1970s and 1980s?
