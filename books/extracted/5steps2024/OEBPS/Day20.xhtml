Day 20

“And … you are to serve your owners with cheerfulness, respect, and humility, not grumbling, or giving any saucy answers, but doing your work with readiness, mildness, and good nature; because your sauciness and grumbling is not so much against your owners, as it is against God himself, who hath placed you in that service, and expects you will do the business of it as he hath commanded you.”

—Thomas Bacon, Sermon to Negro Slaves, 1743

Thomas Bacon was a minister in Maryland. What point is Bacon making to his congregation? What does this tell you about the attitudes of some colonists in eighteenth-century Maryland?
