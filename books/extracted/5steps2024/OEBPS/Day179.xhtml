“Now, as I said in my speech on the economy yesterday, it makes no sense to expel talented young people who, for all intents and purposes, are Americans. They’ve been raised as Americans, understand themselves to be part of this country. To expel these young people who want to staff our labs or start new businesses or defend our country simply because of the actions of their parents or because of the inactions of politicians—in the absence of any immigration action from Congress to fix our broken immigration system, what we’ve tried to do is focus our immigration resources in the right places. So we prioritize border security, putting more boots on the southern border than at any time in our history.”

—President Barack Obama, “Transcript of Obama’s Speech on Immigration Policy,” New York Times, June 15, 2012
