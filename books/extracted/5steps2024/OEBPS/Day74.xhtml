Day 74

D. W. Griffith’s 1915 film The Birth of a Nation is an epic recounting of the Civil War and Reconstruction. This was the first great American feature film, and for many technical and artistic reasons it is a landmark in the history of cinema. Unfortunately, Griffith’s film was controversial even in 1915 for its portrayal of Reconstruction. The heroes at the end of the movie are members of the Ku Klux Klan rescuing white Southerners from freedmen and carpetbaggers. The film can be seen in whole or in part at Youtube.com. What does this film tell us about changes in our understanding of Reconstruction? How does it illustrate changes in our understanding of race relations?
