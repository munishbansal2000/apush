- Diffusion does ONE thing: organic ambient motion on a still. Script (code) does
  everything else (camera moves, text, maps, counters, assembly).
- AI prompts are ambient-motion ONLY: no people/animals in motion, no camera movement
  in the prompt frame, no adding/removing/changing scene content. Enforced by
  `check_prompt_safety()` in `video/animate_still.py` (now negation-aware; see below).
- Every AI prompt ships with its duration attached. Clips are 10s each, silent by design.

  never wording (`video/TRANSCRIPT-CONVERGENCE-u1.md` observed this rule strictly).

  the fallback (6s cap, crossfade-looped to 10s by `ai_clip_scene` in `video/motion.py`).

- Terse turns are direction: "Good" = approved, "Go" = build it, "Stuck?" = status pulse.
