## Quality standards (consolidated)
1. **Prompt safety** (`check_prompt_safety`): ambient-only, static camera, no people/
   animals in motion, no add/remove/change. Negation-aware; camera moves reject.
   Enforced at LTX generation AND in `video_pipeline` clips stage (both providers).
2. **Copyright**: zero tolerance. Competitor transcripts → structure/ideas only, never
   wording. Bank items → re-conceived (same topics/skills/ids, redesigned angles).
   Pre-1930 primary sources quotable verbatim (PD); post-1929 paraphrased.
3. **Gates**: schema-strict (no unknown fields), unique scene/lesson IDs, referenced
   files must exist, text-layout collision-free, final MP4 probed (streams, resolution,
   duration ±2%, no ≥0.75s black runs).
4. **Content bar**: every lesson spec names its grounding (CB released / bank / catalog
   image); no source-less sections; honest gap flags where coverage is missing.
5. **No slop**: no history/shims/junk in builds; example files stay out of the repo
   (local/chat only); Windows scripts pure ASCII.

## Standing laws (do not re-litigate)
- Diffusion does ONE thing: organic ambient motion on a still. Script (code) does
  everything else (camera moves, text, maps, counters, assembly).
- AI prompts are ambient-motion ONLY: no people/animals in motion, no camera movement
  in the prompt frame, no adding/removing/changing scene content. Enforced by
  `check_prompt_safety()` in `video/animate_still.py` (now negation-aware; see below).
- Every AI prompt ships with its duration attached. Clips are 10s each, silent by design.
- Zero-tolerance copyright: competitor transcripts contribute structure/ideas only,
  never wording (`video/TRANSCRIPT-CONVERGENCE-u1.md` observed this rule strictly).
- Munish hand-renders AI clips on his cloud tool (native 10s). Local 5090 LTX path is
  the fallback (6s cap, crossfade-looped to 10s by `ai_clip_scene` in `video/motion.py`).
- Prompt length target 55–75 words (encoder truncates past ~128 tokens).
- Terse turns are direction: "Good" = approved, "Go" = build it, "Stuck?" = status pulse.
- Do NOT touch `video/audio/cuba/*` uncommitted state — that's his, leave alone.
