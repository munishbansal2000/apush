# u1-e1 image provenance

All lesson images must be either period artwork/photos (public domain) or
modern editorial photos. No AI-generated imagery. New downloads land here
with their source; pre-existing files keep their names.

## Public-domain downloads (Wikimedia Commons / Library of Congress)

- `cahokia-mcadams-1887.jpg` — Monks Mound engraving from William McAdams,
  *Records of Ancient Races in the Mississippi Valley* (1887). Pre-1929,
  public domain.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cahokia_monks_mound_McAdams_1887.jpg
- `debry-maize-planting.jpg` — "Florida Indians planting seeds of beans or
  maize", engraving after Jacques Le Moyne, published by Theodor de Bry,
  *Brevis Narratio* (1591). Library of Congress LCCN2001695741.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Florida_Indians_planting_seeds_of_beans_or_maize_LCCN2001695741.jpg
- `six-nations-chiefs-1871.jpg` — "Chiefs of the Six Nations at Brantford,
  Canada, explaining their wampum belts to Horatio Hale, September 14,
  1871." Pre-1929, public domain.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chiefs_of_the_Six_Nations_at_Brantford,_Canada,_explaining_their_wampum_belts_to_Horatio_Hale_September_14,_1871.jpg

- `catlin-prairie-meadows-burning.jpg` — George Catlin, *Prairie Meadows
  Burning* (1832). Bison fleeing a prairie fire into the river. Public
  domain. Frame cropped to canvas.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Prairie_Meadows_Burning_G-001461-20111214.jpg
- `catlin-prairie-fire-riders.jpg` — George Catlin, prairie fire with
  riders fleeing under black smoke (1830s, Smithsonian American Art
  Museum 1985.66.374). Public domain.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:George_Catlin_-_Prairie_Meadows_Burning_-_1985.66.374_-_Smithsonian_American_Art_Museum.jpg
- `catlin-bison-hunt-snow.jpg` — George Catlin, *Buffalo Hunt in Snow
  Shoes* (undated, New Britain Museum of American Art). Hunters on foot
  after the herd, matching the "no horses yet" narration. Public domain.
  Frame cropped to canvas.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Buffalo_Hunt_in_Snow_Shoes_by_George_Catlin,_undated,_oil_on_canvas_-_New_Britain_Museum_of_American_Art_-_DSC09169.JPG
- `white-secotan-village-1585.jpg` — John White, watercolor of the Secotan
  village (1585, British Museum). Longhouses, palisade, cornfields,
  ceremony — the eyewitness Eastern Woodlands town. Public domain.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:North_carolina_algonkin-dorf.jpg
- `mitchell-map-1755.jpg` — John Mitchell, *A Map of the British and French
  Dominions in North America* (1755). Shows the full trade geography with
  Native nations labeled (Sioux, Osages, Iroquois, Chicasaw). Public
  domain. 3840px for Ken Burns drift.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mitchell_Map-06full2.jpg
