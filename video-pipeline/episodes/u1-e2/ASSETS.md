## Notes / judgment calls
- **henry-navigator.jpg replaced**: was a photo of the 1960 Monument of the
  Discoveries statue; now the genuine 15th-c. Nuno Gonçalves panel. Same path,
  so the scene plan needs no changes.
- **maize.jpg replaced**: was a cooked corn-on-the-cob photo; now a maize
  field. Same path, no plan changes needed.
- **columbus credit says it outright**: no authentic lifetime portrait of
  Columbus exists; del Piombo (c.1519) is the best-known posthumous image.
- **smallpox**: deliberately chose the healer-tending-the-sick panel over more
  graphic Codex pages — school-safe, historically authentic.
- **lienzo panel**: shows Cortés with Tlaxcalan allies (evangelization scene),
  which is honest about the alliance dynamic; the famous battle panels were
  only available at unusably small sizes.
- Tordesillas kept as the Cantino planisphere (1502, shows the actual line)
  rather than a modern redrawn graphic — the contemporary map is the stronger
  evidence.
- "Source" links above are best-effort canonical Commons file pages; the exact
  file titles used for download are in the Source column link targets.
