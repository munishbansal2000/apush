# u1-e3 image provenance

Same policy as u1-e1: period artwork/photos (public domain) or modern
photos of authentic artifacts. No AI-generated imagery.

## Pre-existing (kept, verified period)

- `potosi-woodcut.jpg` — Cerro del Potosí, woodcut from Pedro Cieza de
  León, *Crónica del Perú* (1553). The silver mountain with San Francisco
  church below. Public domain.
- `potosi-mita.jpg` — Potosí mine labor (ladders, ore baskets, overseer),
  de Bry-school engraving after the Narratio tradition. Public domain.
- `casta-painting.jpg` — "De Español y d'India, Mestisa", 18th-century
  Mexican casta painting. Public domain.
- `lascasas-cruelty.jpg` — Title page, German edition of Las Casas,
  *Umständige warhafftige Beschreibung* (1665). Shows conquest scenes;
  used for the Las Casas/Valladolid beat — NOT the 1598 dogs print.
  Public domain.
- `lascasas-portrait.jpg` — Las Casas portrait (479px; small, panel use
  only). Public domain.
- `caravel-fleet.jpg` — caravels under sail (1920px). NOTE: caravels are
  1490s technology; anachronistic for the 1570–1588 Drake/Armada beats.
  Held for review, likely retired.
