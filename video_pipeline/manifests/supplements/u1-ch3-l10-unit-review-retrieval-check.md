## Stimulus-based MCQ

> *Adapted from the Requerimiento (1513), read aloud to Native peoples before
> hostilities:* "We ask that you acknowledge the Church and the Pope... But
> if you do not do this, we shall make war against you in every way we can."

The excerpt best supports which conclusion about early Spanish expansion?

(A) Spain sought only peaceful conversion of Native peoples
(B) Spanish expansion paired religious demands with the threat of war
(C) Native peoples broadly welcomed Spanish authority
(D) The papacy opposed the Spanish conquests

**Answer: (B).** The document makes submission a religious demand and names
war as the penalty for refusal — faith and force in a single instrument,
the pattern behind both the conquest and the Valladolid debate.
