## Stimulus-based MCQ

> *Testimony of Josephe, a Pueblo Indian questioned by Spanish authorities
> after the revolt (1681):* "The captains ordered that the names of Jesus and
> Mary should nowhere be uttered. He has seen many houses of idolatry which
> they have built, dancing the dance of the cachina, which this declarant has
> also danced."

The actions described in the excerpt best support which of the following
conclusions about the Pueblo Revolt?

(A) It was primarily an attempt to seize Spanish weapons and military supplies
(B) It aimed to restore Pueblo cultural and religious autonomy, not just end mistreatment
(C) It was coordinated by Spanish missionaries sympathetic to the Pueblo cause
(D) It sought to replace Spanish rule with French colonial administration

**Answer: (B).** Burning churches and banning Christian names goes beyond
protesting labor conditions — it is a program to erase the colonial culture
and restore Pueblo ways, which is exactly what Popé ordered.
