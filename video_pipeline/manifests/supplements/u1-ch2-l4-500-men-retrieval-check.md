## Stimulus-based MCQ

> *Adapted from the Florentine Codex, Book 12 (compiled by Fray Bernardino de
> Sahagún from Mexica survivors, c. 1570s):* "The sores covered us, head to
> foot. We could not walk, could not move. We lay starving in our houses.
> There was no one left to care for us, no one left to bury the dead, no one
> left to fight."

The excerpt best supports which conclusion about the fall of Tenochtitlan?

(A) Spanish steel armor was the decisive factor in open battle
(B) Epidemic disease destroyed the Mexica's capacity to organize a defense
(C) The Tlaxcalans fought alongside Cortés primarily for Spanish gold
(D) Spanish cannons breached the causeways and decided the siege

**Answer: (B).** The passage describes smallpox incapacitating the population
so completely that no defense could be organized — disease as the decisive,
ungovernable factor, beyond anything Spanish weapons achieved alone.
