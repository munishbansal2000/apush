# Retrieval Check — Potosí: The Silver Mountain

## 30-second recall

Without looking at your notes: explain why Spain created the mita, and name
one effect of American silver in Europe and one in Asia.

## Stimulus-based MCQ

> *Adapted from Felipe Guamán Poma de Ayala, Nueva corónica y buen gobierno
> (c. 1615):* "They take our men to the mines of Potosí, where the cold and
> the mercury consume them."

The excerpt best supports which conclusion about labor at Potosí?

(A) The mita was a voluntary wage-labor system that workers joined freely
(B) Mine labor under the mita destroyed workers' health and lives
(C) The Inca mit'a continued unchanged after the Spanish conquest
(D) Silver mining at Potosí required no specialized refining process

**Answer: (B).** Guamán Poma describes the mines consuming the men sent
there — the cold and the mercury killing them — which is the human cost the
mita system imposed.
