# Exam Card — How 500 Men Toppled an Empire

*8–10 second silent hold. Designed to be paused/screenshotted. Also shipped
as a static image (NLE).*

## The playbook (positions)

- **I. TURN THE EMPIRE'S ENEMIES INTO YOUR ARMY** — Tlaxcalans vs. Mexica;
  rival factions vs. the Inca.
- **II. CUT OFF THE HEAD** — Seize Moctezuma; execute Atahualpa after the
  ransom. Empires built on one irreplaceable head cannot reorganize.
- **III. LET DISEASE DO THE REST** — Smallpox kills Cuitláhuac (80 days on
  the throne) and Huayna Capac years before Pizarro arrives.

## At stake

**Conquest** — why 500 men could topple an empire of millions. Guns helped;
they were the smallest part of the answer.

## Outcome

Tenochtitlan falls 1521; Cajamarca 1532. Same pattern, twice: the Mexica and
Inca empires fall to the same three-part playbook eleven years apart.

## Dramatization note

Cortés dramatized from his letters; the smallpox witness adapted from the
Florentine Codex, Book 12.
