# Retrieval Check — From Encomienda to Slavery

## 30-second recall

Without looking at your notes: explain in two sentences why the Spanish
turned from Indigenous labor to enslaved African labor. Then name the system
that ranked colonial society by ancestry, and give one way people moved
within it despite the rules.

## Stimulus-based MCQ

> *Adapted from Bartolomé de Las Casas, A Short Account of the Destruction
> of the Indies (written 1542):* "I saw them perish in the mines, in the
> fields, on the roads. Husbands died, wives died, children died. The land
> emptied, and there was no one left to work it."

The excerpt best supports which conclusion about Spanish colonial labor?

(A) The encomienda system protected Indigenous workers from overwork
(B) Mining labor and disease destroyed the Indigenous workforce on Hispaniola
(C) The repartimiento system improved working conditions after 1549
(D) The casta hierarchy determined who was assigned to the mines

**Answer: (B).** Las Casas describes a workforce dying in the mines and
fields until the land itself emptied — the demographic collapse that created
the labor shortage the rest of the lesson traces.
