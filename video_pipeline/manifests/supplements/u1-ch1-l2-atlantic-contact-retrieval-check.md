## Stimulus-based MCQ

> *Adapted from Christopher Columbus's journal, October 12, 1492 (first
> contact at San Salvador):* "They are gentle and know nothing of evil. They
> would make good servants, and I believe they could easily be made
> Christians, for they seem to have no religion of their own."

The excerpt best illustrates which of the following about early Spanish
contact?

(A) The Spanish viewed Native peoples through the twin lenses of labor and
conversion
(B) The Spanish sought only peaceful trade with Native societies
(C) Columbus had realized he reached a new continent
(D) The encomienda system was already established in 1492

**Answer: (A).** "Good servants" and "made Christians" in one breath fuses
the economic and religious motives at first contact. (C) is wrong — he
thought it was Asia; (D) is wrong — encomienda came later; (B) is
contradicted by "servants."
