## Stimulus-based MCQ

> A historian describes a society of the pre-contact Americas: its people
> built multistory adobe dwellings around central plazas in an arid region,
> watered fields of maize, beans, and squash through irrigation canals, and
> organized the labor to maintain those canals through clan networks.

The society described in the stimulus is best identified as

(A) the Iroquois Confederacy of the Northeast woodlands
(B) the Pueblo peoples of the Southwest
(C) the nomadic hunting bands of the Great Basin
(D) the fishing villages of the Pacific Northwest

**Answer: (B).** Multistory adobe villages, irrigation agriculture in an arid
climate, and clan-organized labor are the defining Pueblo adaptations. The
Iroquois (A) built longhouse villages under a consensus confederacy; the
Great Basin (C) and Pacific Northwest (D) followed different subsistence
patterns.
