## Notes on adjudicated items

### barrons-2027-pt2-20 (VERIFIED — auditor error, recorded key stands)

Item: "Carnegie's outlook, as criticized in the cartoon, drew on which idea?"
with the 1892 'Forty-Millionaire Carnegie in his Great Double Role' cartoon as
context. The auditor's blind derivation was (B) 'Survival of the fittest'; the
recorded key is (C) The Gospel of Wealth.

Adjudication: the recorded key is correct. The cartoon's specific criticism
(confirmed by item pt2-17, key A: 'His philanthropy was funded by violating his
workers' rights') is the philanthropist-vs-exploiter double role — precisely the
tension at the heart of Carnegie's own 1889 'Gospel of Wealth' doctrine, which
held that ruthless accumulation was justified because the rich would administer
their fortunes for the community's good. 'Survival of the fittest' describes his
business ruthlessness generally, but the cartoon targets the philanthropy-funded-by-
exploitation outlook, i.e. the Gospel of Wealth. No edit made; key stands.
