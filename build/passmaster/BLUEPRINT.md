## 6. CED verification note

`~/workspace/apush/build/cb-codes.json` cites the Fall 2019 CED (accessed
2026-10-02) and holds 105 topics, 17 skill codes (1.A-6.D), and 3 reasoning
processes. Verified 2026-11-26 against the Fall 2026 CED on AP Central:
the Unit 1 topic list (1.1-1.7, titles as in section 4), the Unit 1 exam
weight (4-6%), and the ~8 class periods match exactly. The skill codes and
reasoning processes are unchanged across CED editions. The full 105-topic
list will be re-verified against the Fall 2026 CED unit by unit as each
unit starts; any mismatch is logged in LOG.md and the blueprint is
corrected before that unit's authoring begins.

## 7. Exam assembly

When all 9 units are complete, the 600 test-pool questions assemble into
10 exams of 60 questions. Each exam must: cover all 9 units in MCQ roughly
at the unit weights (U1 ~3, U2 ~4, U3-U8 ~8 each, U9 ~3 of the 55 MCQ);
carry 3 SAQs from three different periods and one per slot type; carry 1
DBQ (range 1754-1980) and 1 LEQ; total 3h15m. Exam assembly is a later
tranche, not part of any single unit's work.
