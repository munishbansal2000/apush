Look up the definition of muckraker. The term received its modern meaning from President Theodore Roosevelt in a speech on April 14, 1906. While Roosevelt approved of journalistic exposures of corruption, he cautioned that too many reporters could not tear themselves away from raking muck, like a character in John Bunyan’s Pilgrim’s Progress (1678). Despite, Roosevelt’s criticism, the term muckraker soon became a source of pride to investigative journalists. Why were muckrakers important in the Progressive Era?
