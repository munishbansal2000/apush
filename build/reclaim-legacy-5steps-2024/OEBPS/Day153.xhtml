“Increasingly … government has been absorbing or controlling more and more of our resources, our energy, and our ambition. Today you work from January through April just to provide government with the money it spends. Until early February you are working to pay the expenses of local and state government. For twice as long thereafter you are working to pay the expenses of the Federal Government. Only then do you work for money that you yourself can use for what you yourself choose.

This cancerous growth of the Federal Government must and shall be stopped.”

—Senator Barry Goldwater, Opening Campaign Speech, September 3, 1964
