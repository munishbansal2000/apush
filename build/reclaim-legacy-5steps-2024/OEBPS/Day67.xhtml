Day 67

“But slavery is the cause of the war. Why? Because the South obstinately and wickedly refused to restrict or abolish it at the demand of the philosophers or fanatics or demagogues of the North and West. Then, sir, it was abolition, the purpose to abolish or interfere with and hem in slavery, which caused disunion and war. Slavery is only the subject, but Abolition the cause of this civil war. It was the persistent and determined agitation in the free States of the question of abolishing slavery in the South, because of the alleged ‘irrepressible conflict’ between the forms of labor in the two sections … that forced a collision of arms at last. …”

—Congressman Clement Vallandigham, January 14, 1863

What point is Congressman Vallandigham making? Why is his perspective important?
