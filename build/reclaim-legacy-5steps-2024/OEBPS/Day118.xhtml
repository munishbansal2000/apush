Day 118

“The expenses of government reach everybody. Taxes take from everyone a part of his earnings and force everyone to work for a certain part of his time for the government. … I want the people of America to be able to work less for the government—and more for themselves. I want them to have the rewards of their own industry. This is the chief meaning of freedom. … When the government effects a new economy, it grants everybody a life pension with which to raise the standard of existence. It increases the value of everybody’s property, raises the scale of everybody’s wages. One of the greatest favors that can be bestowed upon the American people is economy in government.”

—President Calvin Coolidge, August 11, 1924

What is President Coolidge’s point in this speech? What does this say about the governing philosophy of the Republican presidential administrations of the 1920s?
