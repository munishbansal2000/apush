Day 12

“Men’s consciences ought in no sort to be violated, urged, or constrained. And whenever men have attempted anything by this violent course, whether openly or by secret means, the issue has been pernicious, and the cause of great and wonderful innovations in the principallest and mightiest kingdoms and countries.”

—Roger Williams, The Bloudy Tenent of Persecution, for Cause of Conscience (1644)

What point is Williams making in this passage? Why is this important for American history?
