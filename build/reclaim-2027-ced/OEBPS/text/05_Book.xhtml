How to Use This Book

Barron’s AP U.S. History is designed to help you master key historical content while developing the analytical and historical thinking skills required for success on the AP U.S. History Exam. You can use this book alongside your course or as a comprehensive review tool as you prepare for the exam.

Course Content

This book provides a thorough review of AP U.S. History, organized to support both content mastery and skill development. You may work through the book sequentially as the course progresses or focus on specific periods and themes as needed. Features of this edition include:

  • ■DA clear overview of the AP U.S. History course framework and exam format

  • ■Review chapters organized by historical periods

  • ■Key terms and concepts emphasized throughout each chapter

  • ■Practice questions that reinforce historical thinking skills, including causation, comparison, and continuity and change over time

As you move through each chapter, review the content carefully before attempting the practice questions to assess your understanding.

Multiple-Choice Questions

Multiple-choice questions on the AP U.S. History Exam assess your ability to analyze historical sources and apply historical reasoning rather than recall isolated facts. In this book:

  • ■End-of-chapter and practice test questions reflect the style and rigor of the exam

  • ■Questions require you to interpret historical texts, visuals, and data

  • ■A dedicated chapter provides strategies for approaching multiple-choice questions effectively

Use these questions to identify strengths and target areas that need additional review.

Free-Response Questions

The AP U.S. History Exam includes free-response questions that assess your ability to analyze historical evidence and construct well-reasoned arguments. This book includes:

  • ■An overview of short-answer, long-essay, and document-based questions

  • ■Sample questions with explanations

  • ■Guidance on how responses are scored, including the use of the AP scoring rubrics

Practice outlining and writing responses, and review the scoring guidance to understand how to earn points efficiently.

Practice Exams

The final section of the book offers the opportunity to take two full-length practice tests that include the question types found on the AP U.S. History Exam. It is recommended that you time yourself when taking these exams so that you become familiar with the pacing required on test day.

Each practice test is followed by detailed answer explanations for the multiple-choice questions and scoring guidance for the free-response questions. Review these explanations carefully to better understand both the content and the reasoning required for high-scoring responses.

Online Practice

In addition to the practice tests included in this book, three additional full-length practice exams are available online. You may take these exams in untimed practice mode or in timed mode. All questions include answer explanations, allowing you to further assess your readiness and refine your exam strategy.
