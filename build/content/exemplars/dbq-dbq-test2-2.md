## Essay

America was kind of imperialist between 1890 and 1945. [Thesis +1, at the floor: "kind of imperialist" is a defensible claim that responds to the prompt, so it earns — but it offers no line of reasoning and no sense of *extent*, which is what the prompt asks the student to evaluate.]

An American official wrote that Filipinos needed "proper care and direction" and that "these people will become happy, and a strength to our nation" (Doc. 5). This shows that Americans thought they were helping the Filipinos by ruling them. [Evidence 0: quoted, not used — and one of only two documents in the essay. Restating the quote adds no understanding of how paternalism functioned as imperial ideology, and two quoted documents fall short of the three-document minimum for describing content.]

A cartoon from 1899 showed Uncle Sam telling McKinley to "finish the canal, McKinley, and make our national expansion complete" with American flags over the Philippines, Hawaii, Cuba, and Puerto Rico (Doc. 7). This shows Americans wanted to expand. America fought Spain in 1898 and got the Philippines, which made it an imperialist country. [Evidence beyond documents +1: the 1898 war with Spain — a specific, relevant outside fact, and the essay's strongest sentence.]

In conclusion, America was kind of imperialist because it expanded and ruled other places.

[Contextualization 0: the essay never reaches beyond the documents — nothing about the European scramble, industrial markets, or the 1898 war's origins. One or two background sentences would earn this point.]

[Sourcing 0 and Complexity 0: the essay never considers why the official or the cartoonist framed empire as benevolence, and it never qualifies "kind of" into an actual extent judgment — for instance, by weighing the anti-imperialist opposition that existed inside the United States itself.]
