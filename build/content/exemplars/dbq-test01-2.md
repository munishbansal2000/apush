**Prompt:** To what extent did the Federalist administrations of George Washington and John Adams promote national unity and advance the authority of the federal government in the period 1789–1801?

**Target score: 2/7.** (Bracketed notes are teacher margin annotations, not student text.)

The Federalist administrations promoted national unity and made the federal government more powerful. So the Federalists tried to keep the country united and gave the federal government more authority. [Evidence +1 (first point): the content of three documents is accurately described.
