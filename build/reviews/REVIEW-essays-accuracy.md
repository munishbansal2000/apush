## Checked and dismissed (no finding)

- `leq-5s24-exam2-leq-02-4.md` (4/6): "the formation of the first party system in the 1780s and 1790s" mirrors
  the test prompt itself ("shaped the emergence of political parties in the 1780s and 1790s").
  The essay echoes the prompt's periodization; not a builder error, no annotation change needed.
- `dbq-5s24-exam2-dbq-4.md` (4/7): "In *Smyth v. Ames*, it declared that 'corporations are persons' under
  the Fourteenth Amendment." Verified against the opinion (169 U.S. 466): Harlan's opinion does state
  "A railroad corporation is a person within the meaning of the Fourteenth Amendment." Accurate — no fix.
- `dbq-barrons-2027-pt2-dbq-7.md` (7/7): "labor journalist Joseph McDonnell" — real: Joseph McDonnell,
  Irish-born socialist, wrote "Another Defense of Chinese Immigration" (1878) against the "Chinese Must Go" cry
  (Digital History). Accurate — no fix.
- `leq-5s24-exam1-leq-02-6.md` (6/6): "enslaved Africans had outnumbered whites since 1708, reaching about
  sixty percent of the population by 1715" — matches accepted history (Black majority from 1708; ~60% by 1715).
  Accurate — no fix.
- `leq-leq-test2-spare-a-6.md` (6/6): "building a planter aristocracy atop a Black majority workforce" —
  refers to Virginia's plantation labor force (overwhelmingly enslaved), not the colony's whole population.
  Defensible reading — no fix.
