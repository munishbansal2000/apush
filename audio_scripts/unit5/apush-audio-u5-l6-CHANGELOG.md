## Factual corrections vs the locked v1
1. **"Not a single Southern electoral vote" → refined.** Britannica:
   Lincoln won zero popular votes in ten of the eleven future
   Confederate states; Virginia the lone exception (~1%). The script
   teaches the popular-vote version (Tier-2 exact), not the
   electoral-vote shorthand. New registry entry F-U5-045.
2. **"Not on the ballot in ten Southern states" → reframed as votes.**
   Britannica frames it as votes won, not ballot access. Script teaches
   "zero popular votes in ten of the eleven states." F-U5-046.
3. **"No one killed in the actual battle" → completed honestly.**
   Princeton Tier-1: "No one died in this first battle" (the
   bombardment). The surrender-salute accident (Pvt. Daniel Hough killed,
   Pvt. Edward Galloway mortally wounded) is broad-web-corroborated,
   not Tier-1/2 — taught with Sources-footer disclosure. F-U5-047.
4. **"Transcripts say a unified Democratic ticket still loses" → CUT.**
   The claim rests on the Unit 5 review transcript alone; it is an
   untestable counterfactual and "technically true but misleading" under
   the Layer-3 framing rule (the premise — a combinable opposition —
   is historically impossible). Cut per fail-closed; the box teaches
   Princeton's verified version instead (the North held the electoral
   majority; a clean sweep wins it). F-U5-044.
5. **"The disease is democracy" / immigrant-voting demographics → CUT.**
   Unverifiable color from v1. Replaced with Tier-1 trend facts: 18 free
   vs 15 slave states, MN/OR free 1858/1859 (transcript), free-state
   electoral majority (princeton), slavery "needed to grow" to stay
   viable (premium2027).
6. **"Five times the population" → never taught.** No population
   multiplier appears anywhere in v5 (registry F-CIV-001).
7. **"Fifty Southern delegates walk out" → number dropped.** 5steps
   Tier-1 verifies the walkout, not the count. Script says "they walk."
8. **"Republicans meet in Chicago" → city dropped.** Not Tier-1/2
   verified. Script says only that the Republicans nominated Lincoln.
9. **Buchanan "does nothing" → replaced with his actual position.**
   His December 1860 message to Congress: secession has no legal right,
   but the federal government has no power to coerce a state back —
   "words, no force." Carried by the primary text (public domain),
   disclosed in the footer. F-U5-050.
10. **Crittenden taught as mechanism + disclosed content.** Princeton
    Tier-1 verifies the compromise existed and died when Lincoln refused
    to soften the no-expansion demand. The Kentucky attribution and the
    36°30′-to-the-Pacific content are beyond Tier 1+2 — taught with
    Sources-footer disclosure. F-U5-048.
11. **Upper-South order made exact.** Sumter (Apr 12) → Lincoln's 75,000
    call ("cause the laws to be duly executed," premium2027) → Virginia,
    Arkansas, North Carolina, Tennessee secede. premium2027 lists the
    four states; the call-then-secession order follows the standard
    account. Never inverted. F-U5-051.
12. **Douglas's result taught as the popular/electoral trap.**
    ~30% popular (2nd) but 12 electoral votes (last): Missouri + part of
    NJ (premium2027; Britannica). Doubles as Maya's wrong beat and the
    box-2 common-mistake line. F-U5-052.
13. **Cornerstone Speech kept short and disclosed.** The quote is cut to
    ~25 words; wording follows the contemporary newspaper report,
    corroborated by the Tier-1 Unit 5 review transcript; footer notes it
    as reported wording. The Mississippi declaration verbatim
    ("Our position is thoroughly identified with the institution of
    slavery") is Tier-1 via premium2027. F-U5-053.
14. **Lincoln's 1860 position both-sided.** premium2027: opposed slavery
    his whole life, but as a 1860 candidate he "would not nor could not
    tamper with slavery where it already existed" — containment, not
    abolition. Consistent with F-U5-040 (never teach 1860 Lincoln as an
    abolitionist). F-U5-054.
