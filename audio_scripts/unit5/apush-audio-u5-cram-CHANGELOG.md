## Structural decisions (v1/v2 → v3)
- **Eleven questions, not ten** — 1:1 per rebuilt lesson (E1, L2–L11). Each answer
  carries the highest-yield fact + the trap where one exists; no new teaching, no depth.
- **1,740 spoken words + 38s scripted pauses = ~10.5 min experienced.** Header,
  cold-open promise ("in about ten minutes"), and the pause-inclusive count agree.
- **Maya drills; Jay answers and gets corrected.** Five real stumbles (the Proviso
  freed enslaved people out west; Republicans were abolitionists; Freeport won
  Lincoln the presidency; the war was over after July 1863; Wade-Davis was 51%),
  six clean answers. The [2-second pause] is the listener's beat — cold open carries
  the U4 grammar: "You answer out loud before Jay does."
- **The eleven Q&A are the self-test.** Q6 is stimulus-style (Mississippi's secession
  declaration → "What's the point of this source?"). Model answers carry CER logic
  as natural connective tissue — zero spoken labels (G13).
- **Unit thesis** (sharpened from v2's longer sentence): "The slavery question broke
  every compromise, broke the nation. And the peace failed to secure what the war
  had won." Jay restates it; Maya tests it across four beats (Wilmot → Kansas →
  Gettysburg/Vicksburg → 1877).
- **Two DBQ bets with thesis sketches:** (1) Reconstruction success-or-failure —
  the obvious top bet; thesis: constitutional success, political and economic
  failure. (2) Slavery as the central cause of the Civil War — the backup; thesis:
  yes, proved with the seceders' own words, with the both-sided line (tariffs and
  states' rights rode along).
- **Budgets (writer sanity check only):** 0 That's/Here's starters, 6 em dashes,
  6 micro-turns, 0 spoken CER labels, 0 twist pivots, 0 retired phrases,
  0 modern analogies. Two "not X" correction shapes reworded out of the G9 family

1. **"Fifty-four forty or fight" as Polk's slogan** — v2's opening beat. F-U4-076:
   Northern congressmen shouted it AT Polk; Polk settled Oregon at the 49th parallel.
2. **"John Brown kills five at Pottawatomie with broadswords"** — v1/v2 wording.
   Tier 1 (premium2027) says "swords"; "broadswords" is unverifiable (F-U5-019).
3. **"About fifty dead" in Kansas** — v2 pinned it. Tier 1 conflicts (transcript ~50
   vs Princeton 200+); taught as disputed (F-U5-018).
4. **"The South demands a federal slave code"** — v2's Dred Scott beat. UNVERIFIABLE
   in Tier 1+2; cut per fail-closed (F-U5-030).
5. **"2,000 Black officeholders"** — v1/v2's Reconstruction beat. Not in Tier 1;
   replaced with 5steps2024's verified 630/16/1 (F-U5-069).
6. **"Shaw University in 1865"** — v2. No Tier-1/2 source verifies "first Black
   college in the South"; cut (F-U5-065).
7. **"200,000 Black soldiers"** — v2's Emancipation beat. Tier 2 (Britannica/NPS/LOC)
   pins ~180,000 (F-CIV-024).
8. **Wade-Davis "51%"** — v2. False precision; L9 v3 standardized on "a bare
   majority" (new F-U5-090).
9. **Antietam as a flat Union win** — v2 ("Antietam... Opens door for Emancipation"
   with no qualifier). F-CIV-028: tactically a draw, strategically the opening
   Lincoln could claim.
10. **Missouri line "to the Pacific" implication** — v2's Compromise beat never
    corrected it. F-U5-017: Louisiana Purchase only; permitted, never guaranteed.
11. **"Ten questions" / "1844 to 1896" framing / Unit 5-to-6 tease** — v1/v2
    structure. Now eleven questions 1:1 per lesson, 1844–1877 per the rebuild
    brief, and the closer is the unit sign-off only.
