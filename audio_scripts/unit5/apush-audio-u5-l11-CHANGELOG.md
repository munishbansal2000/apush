## Factual corrections v1/LOCKED → v3 (all verified before drafting)
1. **Lynching counts CUT.** The Oct-5 locked draft taught "thousands of lynchings between 1880 and 1930." No Tier-1/2 source found during drafting supports a count. Cut entirely; registry F-U5-085 blocks re-adding a number without Tier-1/2 sourcing.
2. **Harlan dissent — exact wording.** LOCKED paraphrased ("the Constitution is color-blind... separate is inherently unequal"). v3 teaches the Britannica-verified wording ("Our Constitution is color-blind, and neither knows nor tolerates classes among citizens.") and uses it as the stimulus-style self-test question.
3. **Plessy mechanics — test case, not a random arrest.** v3 adds the Tier-2-verified Citizens' Committee (formed 1891, Creole professionals in New Orleans), Albion Tourgée as counsel, Homer Plessy's seven-eighths/one-eighth description (the legal point: the law couldn't define the races it claimed to separate), the 1890 Separate Car Act, the 1892 arrest, the May 18, 1896 7–1 vote with one justice not participating.
4. **"60 years" hedged to "nearly sixty years."** Britannica says "well over half a century." Also added the 80–85% school-funding figure (5steps ch26, framed as early-1950s data) instead of the LOCKED's vague "a fraction of the funding."
5. **Wells story — NPS-grounded.** LOCKED had the press destroyed and "she flees North." v3: 1884 train refusal + lawsuit won then overturned by the Tennessee Supreme Court; 1892 Moss/McDowell/Stewart grocery-store lynchings; editorial; mob destroyed her press while she was in New York (which likely saved her life); stayed in the North; wrote the in-depth lynching report for the New York Age; campaign continued to the White House in 1898.
6. **Atlanta Compromise — no strawmen.** v3 teaches both sides' real arguments: Washington (born into slavery, Tuskegee founder, 1895 Atlanta Exposition speech; critics said accommodation, defenders said he read his room) vs. Du Bois (Harvard-trained historian, called it submissive, coined "The Atlanta Compromise," demanded full social equality now, later helped found the NAACP) vs. Wells's confrontational front (investigating and publishing lynching facts). The iconic "separate as the fingers" line was NOT verified in Tier 1/2 and is not taught.
7. **Grandfather-clause mechanics — pinned claims cut.** The 1866/1867 eligibility-cutoff detail appears only in non-Tier sources; v3 teaches only the Tier-1-verified framing (exempt descendants of men who could vote before Black men could; race-neutral in theory, waived for whites in fact). Registry F-U5-086 notes the cut.
8. **New South — both-sided per Tier 1.** v3 uses the 5steps gloss (textile industry from the 1880s; late-1800s industrial-growth concept) + Maximum Insight transcript (Grady the journalist, Northern-funded mills/mines/factories, Charlotte and Atlanta, farming/sharecropping stayed the primary base) — "layered on, never replaced." Birmingham steel was in no Tier-1/2 source; not taught.
9. **Disclosures in the Sources footer:** white primaries (taught per frozen scope, not independently corroborated in Tier 1/2 — Layer 3 to verify; 1944 case deliberately omitted); church-as-institution (framing, not a checkable claim; Tuskegee and the Free Speech are Tier-1-verified).
