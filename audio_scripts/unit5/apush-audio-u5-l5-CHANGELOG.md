- **Racial views:** both-sided and hedged per the guide — Douglas branded Lincoln as favoring racial equality (Britannica, Tier 2); Lincoln's 1858 position taught as containment-not-equality, anchored by Foner via premium2027 Ch.13 (Tier 1: "never became a principled egalitarian"). No heroes/villains flattening; no verbatim Charleston-debate quote (not Tier-1/2-verified in this draft's source set).
- **The advisors anecdote** ("Lincoln's advisors told him not to ask the Freeport question / larger game"): NOT Tier-1/2-verified — CUT from dialogue entirely rather than hedged. See correction #4.
- **Debate format:** taught as "three hours each" (Britannica, Tier 2). The 60/90/30 breakdown is dropped — not Tier-1/2-verified. See correction #2.
- **Crowds:** hedged at "thousands" — no pinned numbers (not Tier-1/2). See correction #5.
- **Dred Scott:** one continuity sentence in the cold open + one clause stating the trap's premise at Freeport ("neither Congress nor a territorial legislature could keep slavery out" — Britannica, Tier 2). No holdings re-teach.
