## Fact corrections vs v1 (all verified BEFORE drafting; Tier 1/2 cited per claim)
1. **"54-40 or fight" attribution — CORRECTED (new registry F-U4-076).** v1 had Polk running on
   "fifty-four forty or fight." Princeton Tier 1 (045_c009_sup) states it was "not Polk's slogan,
   but one directed at him" — Northern congressmen demanding ALL of Oregon to balance slave Texas;
   Polk refused two land wars and settled at the 49th parallel. v2 teaches the correction as a
   Maya common-mistake line ("I have definitely written 'Polk's slogan' on a quiz before").
2. **Five-states clause — CORRECTED.** v1: "allowed Texas to be split into as many as five states."
   The joint resolution's real text (Avalon/Yale primary text): "new States of convenient size, not
   exceeding four in number, in addition to said State of Texas" — i.e., up to four NEW states
   (five total), only with Texas's consent, with the 36°30' line extended (slavery permitted south,
   prohibited north). v2 has Maya fumble it ("That's a cheat code") and Marcus correct her with
   the real text.
3. **Velasco / Rio Grande — REFRAMED (new registry F-U4-077).** v1: "That was the line in the
   Velasco treaties, the line Texas had held for nine years." Technically-true-but-misleading:
   the Rio Grande boundary appears only in the SECRET treaty signed by a captive Santa Anna;
   Mexico never ratified either treaty. v2 teaches it as Texas's CLAIMED reading vs Mexico's
   repudiation — Tomas: "Texas drew the Rio Grande on a map and called it history, working from
   papers Mexico had repudiated."
4. **Alamo numbers — TIER-ALIGNED.** v1: "189 defenders held for 13 days." Tier 1/2 verify
   "~180–200 defenders" (Britannica Kids: about 180; premium2027: almost 200) and 13 days

   figure was dropped as Tier-unverifiable.
5. **Goliad — CORRECTED.** v1: "four hundred Texan prisoners executed." Tier 1 (premium2027):
   "almost 400 were killed"; Britannica Kids: Fannin and 300+ prisoners massacred. v2: "nearly
   four hundred Texans were executed at Goliad."
6. **99-year indentures — DROPPED.** Tier-1/2-silent. Replaced with the verified frame: Mexico
   banned slavery in 1829 but let immigrant slaveholders keep enslaved labor (Britannica Tier 2);
   premium2027 Tier 1: settlers "routinely flouted Mexican law... in practicing slavery."
7. **"Schoolchildren learn this as the North American intervention" — DROPPED** (unverifiable in
   Tier 1/2, as flagged in the task).
8. **Seguin driven out of Texas — DROPPED** (Tier-silent). Seguin kept only where verified:

9. **Travis "victory or death" letter — DROPPED** (Tier-silent). Santa Anna captured "in a

10. **Annexation mechanics — VERIFIED via US State Dept Office of the Historian + Tier 1:**
    Tyler's April 12, 1844 treaty; Mexico severed diplomatic relations over it; Senate defeated

11. **Polk 1844 — REFRAMED.** v1: "The eighteen forty-four election was basically a referendum."
