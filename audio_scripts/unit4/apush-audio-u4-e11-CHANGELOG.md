- **Boxes went 3 → 4.** v1's three (Awakening / reform pipeline / seekers) couldn't hold the mandated scope. v2: (1) the Awakening, (2) the reform pipeline, (3) the seekers, (4) the freedom fight (abolition's rise + Seneca Falls 1848 as its offshoot beat — Seneca Falls gets full treatment in U4-E13 per the archived E12 v1's closing tease, so it's planted here as one box-level beat, not the episode).

The full immediatist-vs-gradualist debate is U4-E12's territory — the closer teases exactly that ("free every enslaved person now, no compromise, or wind slavery down slow and send free Black Americans to Liberia").
- **Continuity:** cold open honors the U4-E10 closer ("while the nation argued about removal, the churches were filling with a religious explosion") with a single removal sentence — reference, not lesson. Closer teases ONLY U4-E12's abolition debate.
- **Length:** 2,096 spoken words, 74 s scripted pauses, 12.88 min experienced runtime → 13-minute promise. This runs above the observed 11–12.5 min episode range; the four mandated boxes plus the perfectionism link earned it, but layer-2 should confirm the length feels earned, not stuffed.
