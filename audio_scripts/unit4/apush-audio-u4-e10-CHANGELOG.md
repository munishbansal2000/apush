## Format choice: Maya + Marcus interview (v1 was Marcus-solo Story Mode)
Why the interview serves this material better:
1. The episode's required verdict question (policy vs. tragedy vs. both) needs a second voice genuinely wrestling — a solo narrator answering his own verdict question is a lecture wearing a costume.
2. The common student errors are natural Maya beats, not grafted-on corrections: "I thought Jackson—" (Van Buren carried it out), "So the Act ordered the Trail of Tears?" (it authorized negotiation), "Twenty men? For sixteen thousand people?" (treaty legitimacy).
3. The mandated disclosures (apocryphal Jackson line, contested Burnett account, Quatie blanket as family tradition) land as honest conversation — Maya asking "Did Ross lose anyone? On the trail?" and Marcus answering "I can't verify the blanket" — rather than footnotes stapled to a monologue.
4. Prediction beat, CER self-test with real pauses, and the box ritual are native to the interview format.
Dignity guardrails for the somber material: no cute openers, no levity beats anywhere; Maya's wrong beats are substantive (state-vs-nation confusion, Jackson-in-1838), her human moment is empathetic (grandmother's letters → "what do you even pick up first?"), never goofy. Her knows-something beat is the honest question Marcus can't fully answer (the Quatie blanket).
