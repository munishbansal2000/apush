## Deliberately left out, and why

- **USMCA detail** — mentioned only inside the registry (F-U8-045), not the script: the Trump
  box was at weight and the reversals that matter for the polarization story are the ones that
  broke with Obama (JCPOA, Paris) plus the failed ACA repeal.
- **Kavanaugh hearings / 230-judge mechanics** — one line ("more than two hundred thirty federal
  judges, and three Supreme Court justices") is the exam fact; the confirmation-fight narrative
  would need book sourcing the Tier-1 excerpts don't give it.
- **BLM (2013) and Obergefell (2015)** — in the Tier-1 timeline but cut from the script: both
  belong to the culture-war backstory, and the episode's four boxes were full. They remain
  available for the unit cram.
- **The "Contract with America"** — used only as the book's own dating anchor ("from the
  Contract with America... straight through to Trump," premium2027 9.1), not re-taught; it was
  covered in its own era.
- **2020 election mechanics / fraud claims** — the script teaches the certified outcome (Biden
  defeated Trump) and moves on; relitigating the count would be punditry, not history, and the
  exam tests the outcome.
- **Any evaluative verdict on the presidencies** — every characterization is either the book's
  own word ("outsider," "rocky") or explicitly attributed ("historians still argue," "critics,"
  "political scientists point to"). The exam cares about causes and patterns, not team scores.
- **No second mid-episode check-in, no duet beat, no paralanguage** — the episode already carries
  a wrong beat, two fumbles, two prediction beats, and a fast bonus; restraint keeps the runtime
  honest.
