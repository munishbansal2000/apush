## Structure decisions
- Three boxes per brief: (1) Gorbachev's reforms — glasnost/perestroika, the
  rescue-that-killed-the-patient; (2) the 1989 cascade — Poland/Solidarity,
  Hungary, Baltic Way, Berlin Wall (Nov 9, 1989), Eastern Europe, then the
  August 1991 coup → Yeltsin → December 1991 dissolution folded as box 2's
  tail; (3) the verdict debate (Reagan pressure vs internal rot), both sides
  taught.
- Continuity: cold open nods U8-L1's closer ("the fight over whether Reagan's
  pressure ended it" — quoted from the U8-L1 script tail); closer teases ONLY
  U8-L3 (1990s globalization & tech). No Reaganomics re-teach, no 1990s drift.
- Two prediction beats ("Your turn, listener," [9-second pause] ×2): the free
  press's first move; Reagan's SDI-or-disarmament choice. Both reasoning, not
  recall.
- One mid-episode box check-in only ("One box down. Gorbachev's reforms,
  landing."). Recap is the check layer.
- Wrong beat (mid-episode): Maya casts Gorbachev as a secret saboteur;
  Marcus corrects firmly ("a believer trying to save the system"). Recap
  fumble (different tool): Maya swaps glasnost/perestroika and catches
  herself mid-landing.
- Maya-knows-something: the Schabowski press-conference clip from her history
  class — she beats Marcus to the punchline ("he just says —"). Marcus names
  him and fills the framing. One personal image: the uncle's "restructured"
  restaurant (the rescue-that-killed-the-patient as family lore).
- Exam devices, all substantive: one common-mistake line per box (Gorbachev as
  saboteur; Wall dated to Reagan's last year; "Reagan won the Cold War" as a
  full answer), varied exam-tip templates (match-the-word-to-the-job;
  why-89-not-earlier; hand-it-the-argument).
- Self-test: 3 questions, all neutral (no tags), all natural CER with no
  spoken labels. Q1 stimulus-style (1987 Soviet cartoon, Chernobyl filing
  cabinet — invented stimulus, marked as a cartoon). Q2 SAQ-shaped (why 1989
  not 1956/1968). Q3 the verdict debate, both-sides answer.
- Closer: shared two-voice landing, new words ("It ended with an unlocked
  press, an open border, and a memo nobody read.").
