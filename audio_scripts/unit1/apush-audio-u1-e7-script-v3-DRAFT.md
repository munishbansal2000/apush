Marcus: It never even got printed in Spain during his lifetime. Las Casas helped make sure of that. But the argument circulated anyway. The conquistadors bought it. Marcus: Then the just-war part. Idolatry and human sacrifice, he argued, offended natural law. So a war that ended those practices, in his telling, wasn't cruelty — it was mercy. Conquest, he said, brings the true faith and real law to people who can't govern themselves. Order through conquest.
