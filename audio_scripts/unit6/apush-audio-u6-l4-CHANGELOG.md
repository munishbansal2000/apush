## Verified (taught flat, Tier-1/2 confirmed)
- Fourteen Points: Jan 8, 1918, Congress address; no secret treaties, free seas, lower trade barriers, fewer weapons, fair colonial settlement, self-determination, League (point 14); Allies accepted as war aims (Britannica; 5steps2024 ch21).
- Paris: conference Jan 1919 (5steps: Jan 12); Wilson seven months in Paris; Big Four — Wilson, Clemenceau (France), Lloyd George (Britain), Orlando (Italy) (Britannica); Wilson traded points to save the League, expecting it to fix the rest later (5steps).
- Treaty signed June 28, 1919, Hall of Mirrors, in effect Jan 10, 1920 (Britannica); League covenant embodied in the treaty — part one (Britannica); Article 231 "war guilt clause" (Britannica); German delegates excluded, sign-or-face-invasion (5steps); ~10% of European territory lost (5steps; State Dept); Rhineland demilitarized (premium2027 ch9); Alsace-Lorraine to France (Britannica); eastern lands to Poland (Britannica); Danzig/Saar under League administration (State Dept).
- Senate fight: two-thirds required (5steps); Lodge (MA, Foreign Relations chair) led reservationists — Monroe Doctrine + Congress-only authorization of League military action (5steps); Article 10 as the war-power flashpoint (Britannica); ~12 irreconcilables led by Borah of Idaho, no to everything (5steps; premium2027); Wilson refused compromise, ordered Democrats to vote no on the reservations version (5steps; premium2027).
- Stroke/tour: Sept 1919 tour, 39 speeches in three weeks, breakdown Sept 25, Oct 2 stroke (partial paralysis, partial blindness, impaired judgment), Edith controlled access, never fully functioned again (Britannica).
- Outcome: votes Nov 19, 1919 and Mar 19, 1920 failed two-thirds (Britannica); US never joined the League; separate peace under Harding, 1921 (5steps; Britannica); rejection ≠ isolationism — US active in world affairs through the 1920s (5steps).
- League record: established Jan 10, 1920, disbanded Apr 19, 1946, powers/functions transferred to UN (Britannica); "lacked teeth," unanimity veto (Britannica); US failed to ratify Mar 1920; Germany 1926–33, Japan withdrew 1933, Italy 1937 (Britannica); Manchuria invaded 1931 (premium2027); Abyssinia sanctions Oct 1935 excluded oil/iron/steel, lifted within a year, not applied for Rhineland 1936 (Britannica); settled small disputes (Finland–Sweden etc.); UN "formed a continuum" with the League (Britannica).
