## Verified (taught flat, Tier-1/2 confirmed)
- Selective Service: ~24M registered, ~2.8M drafted of ~4.8M served (HISTORY, army.mil, 1914-1918-online encyclopedia); Act signed May 1917; first drawing July 1917 (v1's date stands).
- WIB/Baruch, Food Administration/Hoover, "Food Will Win the War," meatless Mondays/wheatless Wednesdays (premium2027 ch9; NPS; NARA).
- Four-Minute Men ~75,000 (1914-1918-online encyclopedia, peer-reviewed).
- Great Migration: 500,000+ Black Southerners left the South during the war (Princeton ch11); Chicago Black population more than doubled 1910–1920 (census-share 2.0%→4.1%).
- NAWSA >2M members by 1917; NWP founded by Alice Paul 1916; Wilson backed suffrage 1918; amendment through Congress 1919, ratified 1920 (premium2027 ch9; H-Net).
- Espionage Act 1917 (draft, bond sales, "disloyal" speech); Sedition Act 1918 (extended to government/flag/military) (premium2027 ch9; Princeton ch11).
- Schenck: Socialist organizer, mailed anti-draft leaflets, argued draft = involuntary servitude (13th Amendment), Holmes's "falsely shouting fire in a theatre," "clear and present danger," convicted 1919 (premium2027 ch9; Princeton ch11).
- Debs: 10-year sentence for 1918 speech (Espionage Act), ran 1920 from Atlanta federal penitentiary, Wilson refused pardon, Harding commuted, released Christmas Day 1921 (Brandeis/HISTORY).
