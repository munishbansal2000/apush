  L1+L2's containment lineage folded into a single opening beat. Each answer carries

  cold-open promise ("in about eleven minutes"), and the pause-inclusive count agree.

  MacArthur's firing, Rosenberg year, missile-crisis year, Medicare flip, malaise speech),
  five clean runs. The [2-second pause] is the listener's beat — cold open carries the
  U6 grammar: "You answer out loud before Marcus does."
- **One prediction beat** (beat 6: air strikes vs naval blockade — "which one is the
  act of war?", [8-second pause]) at the natural decision point.
- **Thesis test replaces the box recap** (cram format, per U6): "Containment was the
  foreign policy; the Cold War was the domestic one." Marcus tests it against the beats;
  Maya repeats it; the self-test repeats the content.
- **Self-test: three AP-shaped questions** — Q1 stimulus-style (U-2 photo → "what's the
  point of this source?"), Q2 CER (why the CRA didn't finish the movement's work),
  Q3 fast (police-action label logic). Model answers carry CER logic in natural phrasing —
  no spoken Claim/Evidence/Reasoning labels (G13). Pauses 18s / 15s / 8s.

  line remade America") + the house sign-off. No Unit 8 tease.
