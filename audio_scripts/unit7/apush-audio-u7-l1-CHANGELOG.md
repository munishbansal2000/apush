## Factual corrections (with sources)
1. **NAM birth flattened in v3.** v3: "The Non-Aligned Movement is born" at
   Bandung 1955. Corrected: Bandung (April 18–24, 1955, 29 countries) was the
   precursor; the movement was formally established 1961 in Belgrade. Maya's
   recap fumble now teaches the 1955/1961 split as a timing trap. Sources:
   Britannica (Bandung Conference article) + broad-web cross-checks.
   → Registry F-U7-001.
2. **Poland 1947 election now precise.** v3: "a landslide nobody outside Moscow
   believes." v2 keeps the line but pins it: January 1947, Democratic Bloc
   officially ~80%, widely regarded as falsified (opposition suppressed).
   Sources: 5steps ch25 ("the free and fair elections... did not materialize")
   + broad-web cross-checks. → Registry F-U7-002.
3. **Princeton discrepancy CONFIRMED as a book error: Long Telegram origin.** Princeton's text
   says Kennan sent it from "his duty station in Germany." Britannica's Kennan article and the telegram's own
   heading ("The Chargé in Moscow (Kennan)," Feb 22, 1946) confirm Moscow, February 1946; the script teaches
   Moscow. The coordinator independently verified this. → Registry F-U7-003.
4. **Soviet WWII deaths hedged.** v3: "Twenty-six, twenty-seven million dead."
   v2: "roughly twenty-seven million" with the explicit hedge that the exact
   number is debated (26.6M Russian Academy of Sciences; old Soviet official
   figure was 20M — never pin 20M). Sources: broad-web cross-checks.
   → Registry F-U7-004.
5. **Yalta/Potsdam elections-promise split.** New explicit guard: the free-
   elections promise belongs to Yalta (Feb 1945); Potsdam (July 1945) made no
   new promise. Common-mistake line + exam tip built around it. Source: 5steps
   ch25. → Registry F-U7-005.
6. **Iron Curtain "coined" avoided.** 5steps' glossary says Churchill "coined"
   the term; the phrase predates him. v2: "gives the split its famous image."
   Source: 5steps ch25 (Fulton, Missouri, March 1946). → Registry F-U7-006.
