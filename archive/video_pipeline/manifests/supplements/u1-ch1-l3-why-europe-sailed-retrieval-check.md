## Stimulus-based MCQ

> *Adapted from the Treaty of Tordesillas (1494):* "All lands east of the
> line drawn through the Atlantic shall belong to Portugal; all lands west
> of the line shall belong to Spain. What each crown finds within its share,
> each may claim."

The agreement in the excerpt most directly reflects which of the following?

(A) A religious effort to convert Indigenous peoples to Christianity
(B) Rival Catholic crowns dividing colonial claims by bilateral treaty
(C) Portugal's abandonment of its African trade route after 1498
(D) Spain's decision to fund Columbus's western voyage

**Answer: (B).** The treaty is Spain and Portugal settling their rivalry by
drawing a line across the ocean themselves — diplomacy serving empire, not
mission. (A papal bull, *Inter Caetera*, had drawn a line in 1493, but the
two crowns superseded it with their own treaty.)
