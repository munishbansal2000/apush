## Stimulus-based MCQ

> *A field of potatoes could feed three times as many people as a field of
> wheat, and potatoes grew on land too wet, too dry, or too poor for wheat.*

This observation best supports which conclusion about the Columbian Exchange?

(A) American crops increased the number of people Afro-Eurasia could feed
(B) European wheat failed as a crop in the Americas
(C) The potato directly caused the Industrial Revolution
(D) Maize replaced wheat across Europe

**Answer: (A).** The mechanism is carrying capacity: more calories per acre,
on acres wheat could not use — so the same land fed more people.
