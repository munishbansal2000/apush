# Exam Card — Why Europe Sailed West

*8–10 second silent hold. Designed to be paused/screenshotted. Also shipped
as a static image (NLE).*

## The chain (positions)

- **I. THE PRIZE** — Asian spices crossed Islamic lands and Italian
  city-states; every middleman took a cut. A sea route would skip them all.
- **II. THE MEANS** — Portugal: Prince Henry's voyages, the caravel, the
  astrolabe; Dias rounds the Cape (1488), da Gama reaches India (1498).
  Spain: the Reconquista ends at Granada (1492), freeing a crusading state.
- **III. THE DIVISION** — Columbus's wrong math wins three ships; landfall
  October 12, 1492. Tordesillas (1494): east of the line Portugal, west
  Spain.

## At stake

**AP Topic 1.2** — why European rulers paid for voyages into an unknown
ocean, and how they divided what they found.

## Outcome

Gold first: the profit motive launched the ships; God and glory rode
along.

## Dramatization note

The navigator's log (1488) and the herald's reading of Tordesillas are
dramatized from the historical record, not verbatim quotes.
